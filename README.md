# Business Analytics Dashboard
A Power BI solution built for a client to analyze and visualize business performance across their portfolio of hotels, people operations, and chain sustainability management.

# Overview
This project was developed for a client seeking a clear, interactive view of their business analytics. Data was sourced from a live Google Sheets feed, cleaned and transformed in Power BI, and used to build dashboards that help the client understand performance across their various operations.

# What's Covered
- Hotel management — performance metrics across the client's hotel properties
- People analytics — workforce and personnel insights
- Chain sustainability management — tracking sustainability metrics across the business chain

# Workflow
1. Connected to the client's online Google Sheets data source
2. Processed and modeled the data in Power BI (cleaning, transformation, relationships)
3. Built interactive visuals and dashboards to surface key business insights

# Purpose
To give the client a single, understandable view of their business analytics — turning raw spreadsheet data into actionable insights across hotels, people, and sustainability operations.
